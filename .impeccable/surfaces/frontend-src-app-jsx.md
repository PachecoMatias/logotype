# Public Site and Project Configurator

## Scope and mode

Public home is a Persuade surface; the project configurator is an Operate flow that inherits the same visual world. The primary audience is a university exhibition visitor or evaluator. The immediate public action is to understand the proposal and open the working configurator; the protected footer action opens the existing internal panel.

## Direction contract

**THESIS** — Logotype is presented as a living release sheet: raw project intent is registered, aligned, and resolved into a working software system. The page refuses the standard agency hero followed by equal cards. Its signature interaction is an oversized `LOGO / TYPE` composition whose layers settle into alignment as the opening narrative resolves.

**OWN-WORLD** — The Engineering Blueprint world borrows the structural honesty of prepress registration proofs and software architecture drawings without imitating a print poster. Frost is the reading ground, graphite is structural ink, blueprint blue marks decisions and hierarchy, systems teal marks verified/live/complete state, and pale technical blue marks selection. Hairlines, crop marks, oversized typographic fragments, and annotated connectors form the component language. Typography pairs a characterful wide grotesk display voice with a restrained humanist sans reading voice, using only the project's permitted font-loading strategy. Sections alternate registered drafting-paper, frost, technical-tint, graphite, and blueprint fields by narrative role; shared edges, inset hairlines, and restrained registration corners create flat structural depth without gradients, glass, generic icon tiles, or repeated card grids.

**STORY** — The visitor first sees a fragmented name become a coherent system, then follows who Logotype is, what it set out to solve, how the team and services organize the response, and how the functional prototype and infrastructure prove the proposal. The configurator turns the same registration idea into a calm guided conversation: each answer locks one layer of the project brief into place before a clear final review.

**FIRST VIEWPORT** — A compact editorial header sits on a ruled top line. The first viewport is dominated by the split wordmark `LOGO` and `TYPE`, set at architectural scale across a frost field with a moving blueprint-blue registration axis and one compact statement crossing the composition. The primary configurator action is embedded in that axis rather than isolated in a generic button block; the beginning of the company narrative is visible below the fold. Motion settles offsets and reveals meaning, never hiding the content by default.

**FORM** — Chosen grounded direction: third of seven, “living release sheet,” assigned by seed `a5d1895f`. Its system combines prepress proof artifacts, architectural alignment, and the repository's real software flow. The strongest challenger discipline retained is the daylight section's continuous spatial reading; the page behaves as one long section rather than disconnected panels. The tensegrity challenger donates visibly connected dependencies. The VU bridge donates synchronized group motion without meter styling. Other challengers were declined because their literal materials would reduce product clarity for an academic software presentation.

**FINISH** — unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

- Preserve every academic content item and Spanish voseo.
