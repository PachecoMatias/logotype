# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is made up of university exhibition visitors and evaluators who need to understand Logotype as a software development company, its academic foundation, and its working prototype. Prospective clients also use the public site and guided configurator to understand the company and describe a software project.

## Product Purpose

Logotype presents the company, its objectives, team structure, services, identified problem, proposed solution, functional prototype, infrastructure, and conclusions. Its guided project configurator collects a prospective project's context through seven ordered steps and sends the existing project request without changing its business contract.

Success means the academic content is complete and credible, the public experience feels intentionally crafted rather than template-generated, visitors can understand the proposal quickly, and the existing configurator and internal request panel remain operational.

## Positioning

Logotype connects an academic software-company presentation with a working, structured project-intake prototype. The site must demonstrate the team's reasoning and implementation rather than rely on generic agency claims.

## Operating Context

The public site is presented during a university exhibition and is expected to work across mobile, tablet, and desktop widths. Visitors move between the public home, the seven-step configurator, and the existing internal panel entry exposed from the footer.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the seven configurator steps in this order: Empresa, Proyecto, Problema, Funcionalidades, Alcance, Presupuesto, Resumen.
- Preserve the configurator payload, validation rules, API behavior, and business state exactly.
- Preserve the public academic content while allowing its presentation order and hierarchy to change.
- Keep the internal requests panel, project detail, AI analysis, backlog, Kanban board, story modal, backend, and APIs outside the redesign scope.
- Use React, plain CSS, and the installed Framer Motion package. Do not introduce Tailwind or a UI library.
- Respect user reduced-motion preferences and keep navigation between home, configurator, and panel working.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Logotype. Public-facing content remains in Spanish with voseo. The identity must feel authored for Logotype and avoid generic AI/SaaS conventions, stock photography, decorative glass effects, excessive shadows, and violet-blue gradients. The Menta Experts site is a motion and editorial-rhythm reference only; its design, layouts, colors, and components must not be copied.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the complete academic copy, organization data, service descriptions, functional prototype narrative, infrastructure explanation, conclusions, and a working configurator and panel. It contains no project imagery; future real-image needs must be represented as clearly marked slots rather than fabricated or stock assets.

## Product Principles

1. Demonstrate the software proposal with concrete academic substance.
2. Preserve working contracts while improving the visitor experience.
3. Use editorial hierarchy and purposeful motion to guide comprehension.
4. Keep the public identity specific to Logotype rather than trend-driven.
5. Make every route and step usable from mobile through desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The interface must support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast, responsive layouts without horizontal scrolling, and the user's reduced-motion preference.
